Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

QuickAsset is a product of Social Stream Manager.

What we collect

  • Account and contact information you provide, including email address and your connected Stripe account identifier.
  • Files you upload and their metadata, including file name, size, and type.
  • Transaction records for sales made through your payment links.
  • Basic usage and diagnostic data, such as pages visited and device or browser information.

How we use it

  • To provide and operate the QuickAsset service.
  • To connect your account to Stripe and support payment processing.
  • To create and operate your payment links.
  • To deliver purchased files and send transactional emails.
  • To support users, prevent abuse, and maintain the service.

Storage and security

QuickAsset uses Vercel for application hosting and file infrastructure and Stripe for payment processing. Connections to QuickAsset and Stripe use encrypted HTTPS connections. QuickAsset does not see or store payment card numbers.

During early access, file delivery uses direct Vercel Blob URLs. Anyone who obtains a delivery URL may be able to access that file, so do not use QuickAsset for highly sensitive or confidential files until stronger file-access controls are in place.

We do not sell your data or your buyers' data. We share information with service providers only as needed to operate QuickAsset, process payments, deliver files, provide support, or comply with legal obligations.

Your data

You can request deletion of your account information and files by emailing engage@socialstreammanager.com.

Contact

If you have privacy questions or requests, email engage@socialstreammanager.com.
